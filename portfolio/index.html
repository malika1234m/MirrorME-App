Core Features

Everything a fashion app needs

Built from scratch — frontend, backend, AI integration, and real-time body tracking all in one system.

🤖

AI Outfit Analysis

GPT-4o Vision detects clothing types, colours, and fashion styles the moment you upload — then gives real stylist tips.

⭐

Community Rating

Tinder-style swipe deck. Rate outfits 1–10, swipe Fire or Pass, and track your outfit's average score from the community.

🔍

Visual Match Search

Upload any photo and AI finds visually similar styles from verified brand catalogs using embedding cosine similarity.

👗

Virtual Wardrobe & Mirror

Browse brand collections and try them on live using MediaPipe Pose body tracking — no app store download needed.

📏

AI Size Recommender

Enter height and weight. The algorithm estimates chest/waist measurements and maps them to XS–XXXL with a confidence score.

🏪

Brand Storefronts

Brands register, upload collections, and get discovered. Admin verification system, follow counts, and product analytics.
